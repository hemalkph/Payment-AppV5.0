Refund & Payment Policy

Applies to all ICT class enrolments on pasinduathukorala.lk — monthly subscriptions and one-time course purchases.

Last updated: May 2026 2 min read
Overview Monthly classes One-time courses Non-refundable items Our responsibility Processing time Contact us

We are committed to providing high-quality ICT education. Because our course materials, LMS access, and class recordings are delivered digitally and immediately upon enrolment, our refund policy reflects the nature of these digital services. Please read this policy carefully before enrolling.

Monthly subscription classes

Each month is treated as a separate enrolment period. Once a monthly payment is made and access is granted, that month is considered active and non-refundable.

Allowed

You may cancel your subscription at any time. No further months will be charged after cancellation.

No refund

Refunds are not available for the current active month once monthly tutes have been sent, LMS access has been provided, or live class access has been granted.

No refund

Partial-month refunds are not issued regardless of how many classes you attended during that month.

One-time full payment classes

These courses require a single upfront payment for full course access. Once any of the following services have been delivered, the payment is considered non-refundable:

  • Monthly tutes or study materials have been dispatched
  • LMS access has been activated (recordings, resources, or live class access)
  • Access to Full Paper classes or mock exams has been granted

Exception: If you request a refund before any materials or access have been provided, a full refund may be issued at our discretion within 48 hours of payment. Contact us immediately if this applies to you.

Non-refundable items

The following are strictly non-refundable under all circumstances:

No refund

Any month where tutes have been sent or LMS access was active during the billing period.

No refund

One-time payment courses where any content, recording, or access has been delivered to your account.

No refund

Full Paper classes or mock exam access once participation or entry has been granted.

No refund

Downloaded or accessed study materials in any format.

Our responsibility

If an issue arises on our end, we will make it right. Qualifying situations include:

Eligible

We failed to provide promised LMS access or class participation after payment was received.

Eligible

A technical error on our platform prevented you from attending or accessing content you paid for.

Eligible

Promised content was not delivered at all. We will investigate and offer a refund or account credit depending on your preference and the situation.

Processing time

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed within 14 business days of approval. The time for the funds to appear in your account may vary depending on your bank or payment provider and is outside our control.

Contact us

Have questions about this policy or want to raise a concern? We are happy to review your situation and work towards a fair outcome. Please reach out with your full name, the course you enrolled in, and the nature of your query.
